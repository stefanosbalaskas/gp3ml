# Motivation letter

Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript **“gp3ml: Governance-First Predictive Modelling and Validation for Gazepoint Research in R”** for publication in *The R Journal*.

The paper addresses a recurring methodological problem in predictive analyses of eye-tracking and other behavioural data: rows are often clustered by participants, stimuli and trials, while preprocessing, model selection and threshold decisions can introduce information leakage or support a narrower generalization claim than the one reported. The manuscript introduces `gp3ml`, an R package that integrates explicit purpose and role declarations, target-aligned grouped and nested resampling, fold-local preprocessing, retained assessment predictions, decision and abstention governance, shift and robustness auditing, portable artefacts, provenance and API contracts.

The contribution is relevant to the R community because it provides an executable governance layer that complements established modelling ecosystems rather than duplicating their learner catalogues. The paper includes a deterministic synthetic case study, generated figures and tables, primary-source-supported software comparison, and scripts that install the repository into a temporary library before rendering. No private participant data are used.

The manuscript has not been published or submitted elsewhere. All code and materials required to reproduce the paper are included in the submission workspace and will be made publicly available with the repository.
